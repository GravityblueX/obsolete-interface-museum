## Ideas that survived

Separate three different claims:

1. **mechanical survival** — connector/cable ideas remain;
2. **protocol/command survival** — commands or software model remain;
3. **ecosystem replacement** — a newer interface simply took over the use case.

Do not infer one from another.

## Migration experience

How did real users move away from the interface: adapters, bridge chips, dual-interface devices, compatibility modes, replacement machines, or abrupt break?

## Dead ends

Record ideas that did *not* survive. A museum should preserve abandoned design choices, not only tell a victory story about descendants.
